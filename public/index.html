About

IC-MES 2027 is a multidisciplinary international platform that brings together researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, innovators, research scholars and students from India and abroad to present and exchange recent advances in emerging engineering and technology.

The conference focuses on the integration of Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Biomedical Engineering, Advanced Manufacturing, 3D Printing, Control Systems, Optimization, Simulation, Robotics, Advanced Materials, Aerospace, Energy and Sustainable Engineering.

It includes keynote lectures, invited talks, panel discussions, poster presentations, industry interaction and research networking, with the aim of building IC-MES into an annual international conference series of Arka Jain University.

Arka Jain University, Jharkhand

Arka Jain University, Jharkhand, is a NAAC Grade A accredited university located in Jamshedpur.

📘 Springer Nature Conference Proceedings Series

Selected and registered high-quality papers will be published in Springer Nature proceedings series such as Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems (LNNS) or Lecture Notes in Mechanical Engineering (LNME).

Both series are submitted for indexing in Scopus, Web of Science (CPCI), EI Compendex, INSPEC, and Google Scholar. All papers will be assigned unique DOI numbers and permanently archived.

Scopus Indexing Target Springer Nature Series Double-Blind Peer Reviewed Assigned DOI & ISBN

Paper Formatting Guidelines

  • Manuscript length: 8 to 12 pages in single-column Springer format
  • Format: Microsoft Word (.docx) or LaTeX templates
  • Figures: High resolution (minimum 300 DPI) in TIFF, EPS, or PNG
  • References: Vancouver or APA reference standard as per Springer template

Proceedings Review Criteria

  • Technical novelty, originality, and industrial relevance
  • Soundness of experimental / theoretical methodology
  • Similarity index strictly below 15% via Turnitin / iThenticate
  • At least two independent peer reviewers' recommendations

Publication Integrity, Ethics & COPE Compliance

IC-MES 2027 strictly adheres to the code of conduct defined by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE). Authors, editors, and reviewers are expected to uphold the highest standards of scientific integrity.

Author Commitments

  • Submissions must represent original, unpublished research
  • Simultaneous submission to other conferences or journals is prohibited
  • All co-authors must have contributed significantly to the research
  • Any AI-assisted writing or generation must be explicitly declared

Anti-Plagiarism Screening

  • All manuscripts are screened via Turnitin / iThenticate prior to review
  • Max permissible similarity index is 15% (excluding references)
  • Self-plagiarism without proper citation is treated as ethical breach
  • Papers violating ethics are immediately withdrawn and blacklisted
