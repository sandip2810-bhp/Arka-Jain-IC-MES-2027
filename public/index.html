About the Conference

IC-MES 2027 brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, research scholars and students from India and abroad to present recent advances in emerging engineering and technology. It includes keynote lectures, invited talks, panel discussions, poster presentations and industry interaction.

Objectives

  • Strengthen the international academic and research profile of Arka Jain University.
  • Build international research collaborations and industry–academia partnerships.
  • Promote high-quality interdisciplinary research and innovation.
  • Give researchers and academicians an international platform to share their latest findings.
  • Promote emerging applications of AI, optimization, simulation and intelligent systems.
  • Encourage young researchers, Ph.D. scholars and students to take part in advanced research.
  • Explore joint publications, patents, funded research and technology transfer.
