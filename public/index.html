Welcome Message from the Organizing Leadership

Arka Jain University • IC-MES 2027

It is our immense pleasure to welcome you to the International Conference on Modeling, Emerging Technologies & Sustainability (IC-MES 2027), a distinguished academic platform dedicated to advancing interdisciplinary research, technological innovation, and sustainable development.

IC-MES 2027 aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, policymakers, and innovators from across the globe to exchange knowledge, share pioneering research findings, and explore emerging trends in modeling, advanced technologies, and sustainability. The conference seeks to foster meaningful dialogue between academia and industry, encourage collaborative research, and promote innovative solutions to contemporary scientific, technological, environmental, and societal challenges.

The conference will provide a vibrant forum for keynote addresses, invited lectures, technical paper presentations, interactive discussions, and industry–academia engagement across diverse areas, including mathematical and computational modeling, artificial intelligence, machine learning, advanced materials, smart manufacturing, renewable energy, the Internet of Things (IoT), digital transformation, and sustainable engineering systems.

With a strong emphasis on innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, and responsible technological advancement, IC-MES 2027 aspires to strengthen global research networks, inspire new ideas, and contribute to a more sustainable and technologically advanced future.

We warmly invite you to participate in IC-MES 2027 and contribute your valuable research, expertise, and perspectives to this collective academic endeavor. We look forward to welcoming you to an enriching and inspiring conference experience that fosters knowledge exchange, professional collaboration, and lasting research partnerships.

About

Arka Jain University, Jharkhand

Arka Jain University, Jharkhand, is a premier private university accredited with NAAC Grade 'A' and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and apex statutory councils including AICTE, BCI, and PCI. Located in the bustling industrial city of Jamshedpur, Jharkhand, the university is recognized for its commitment to academic excellence, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and high-impact research.

The university fosters an inclusive multidisciplinary learning ecosystem equipped with high-tech computational laboratories, research centers, smart classrooms, incubation units, and an expansive academic conclave auditorium. By forging active partnerships with leading global institutions and industries, Arka Jain University equips scholars and researchers to advance pioneering technological solutions for global and societal challenges.

School of Engineering and IT

The School of Engineering and IT, led by Dr. Ashwini Kumar, Dean, is the vanguard of cutting-edge technology, innovative engineering education, and collaborative scientific research at Arka Jain University.

The School offers comprehensive undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral degree programs spanning Computer Science & Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical & Electronics Engineering, and Civil Engineering.

With specialized research clusters, IoT and robotics labs, advanced manufacturing facilities, and active student professional chapters, the School of Engineering and IT continuously drives patents, sponsored research projects, and high-quality international publications, proudly organizing IC-MES 2027.

About the Conference (IC-MES 2027)

IC-MES 2027 is a multidisciplinary international platform that brings together researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, innovators, research scholars and students from India and abroad to present and exchange recent advances in emerging engineering and technology.

The conference focuses on the integration of Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Biomedical Engineering, Advanced Manufacturing, 3D Printing, Control Systems, Optimization, Simulation, Robotics, Advanced Materials, Aerospace, Energy and Sustainable Engineering.

It includes keynote lectures, invited talks, panel discussions, poster presentations, industry interaction and research networking, with the aim of building IC-MES into an annual international conference series of Arka Jain University.

📘 Springer Nature Conference Proceedings Series

Selected and registered high-quality papers will be published in Springer Nature proceedings series such as Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems (LNNS) or Lecture Notes in Mechanical Engineering (LNME).

Both series are submitted for indexing in Scopus, Web of Science (CPCI), EI Compendex, INSPEC, and Google Scholar. All papers will be assigned unique DOI numbers and permanently archived.

Scopus Indexing Target Springer Nature Series Double-Blind Peer Reviewed Assigned DOI & ISBN

Paper Formatting Guidelines

  • Manuscript length: 8 to 12 pages in single-column Springer format
  • Format: Microsoft Word (.docx) or LaTeX templates
  • Figures: High resolution (minimum 300 DPI) in TIFF, EPS, or PNG
  • References: Vancouver or APA reference standard as per Springer template

Proceedings Review Criteria

  • Technical novelty, originality, and industrial relevance
  • Soundness of experimental / theoretical methodology
  • Similarity index strictly below 15% via Turnitin / iThenticate
  • At least two independent peer reviewers' recommendations

Publication Integrity, Ethics & COPE Compliance

IC-MES 2027 strictly adheres to the code of conduct defined by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE). Authors, editors, and reviewers are expected to uphold the highest standards of scientific integrity.

Author Commitments

  • Submissions must represent original, unpublished research
  • Simultaneous submission to other conferences or journals is prohibited
  • All co-authors must have contributed significantly to the research
  • Any AI-assisted writing or generation must be explicitly declared

Anti-Plagiarism Screening

  • All manuscripts are screened via Turnitin / iThenticate prior to review
  • Max permissible similarity index is 15% (excluding references)
  • Self-plagiarism without proper citation is treated as ethical breach
  • Papers violating ethics are immediately withdrawn and blacklisted
